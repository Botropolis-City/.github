# Botropolis

Every Claude Code session on your machine, drawn as a city.
Districts are projects, buildings are sessions, and everything on the map means one thing you can read by hovering it.

[![The city on its island](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Botropolis-City/botropolis/main/docs/screenshots/hero.png)](https://botropolis.city)

- **See it:** [botropolis.city](https://botropolis.city), with the film of every view.
- **Get it:** on Arch, [`botropolis`](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/botropolis) or [`botropolis-bin`](https://aur.archlinux.org/packages/botropolis-bin) from the AUR; on Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora and openSUSE, from [pkg.botropolis.city](https://botropolis.city/#install); on macOS, `brew tap botropolis-city/botropolis https://pkg.botropolis.city/brew`.
- **Read the code:** [botropolis](https://github.com/Botropolis-City/botropolis), the app; [botropolis.city](https://github.com/Botropolis-City/botropolis.city), the website; [meta](https://github.com/Botropolis-City/meta), the roadmap and how each thing on it was decided.
